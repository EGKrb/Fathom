# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — contributors and maintainers of Fathom — pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Using the project to solicit or assist unauthorized intrusion, or other
  conduct that is illegal or clearly unethical.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces (the repository, issues,
pull requests, and Discussions) and when an individual represents the project in
public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported privately to the maintainer via the repository's **Security →
Report a vulnerability** channel, or a minimal issue requesting a contact.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response
appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code
of Conduct, and may ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for behaviors
they deem inappropriate.
